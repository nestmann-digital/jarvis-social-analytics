2. Purpose of the Application

Nestmann Social Analytics is a social media analytics application under development. It is designed to retrieve video performance information from connected social media accounts and organize it in a central dashboard.

3. Data We Process

Depending on the permissions granted, the application may process account identifiers, video identifiers, video URLs, publication information, view counts, likes, comments, and shares.

4. How Data Is Used

Data is used to track video performance, maintain analytics records, and compare content performance over time. The application does not require access to private messages.

5. Third-Party Services

The planned technical infrastructure includes Google Apps Script for automated processing and Notion for organizing analytics data. Relevant data may be transferred to these services when the integration is enabled.

6. Data Retention and Security

Data will be retained only as necessary for the analytics purposes described above. Access credentials will be stored separately from publicly accessible website content. Appropriate access controls will be applied.

7. Your Rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
