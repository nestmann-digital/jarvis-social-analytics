2. Purpose of the Application

AEVION Social Analytics is a private social media analytics application operated exclusively by Oliver Nestmann for analyzing his own authorized social media accounts.

The application collects and organizes video performance metrics in a private analytics dashboard. It does not offer public registration or allow third-party users to connect accounts.

3. Data We Process

Depending on the permissions granted, the application may process account identifiers, video identifiers, video URLs, publication information, view counts, likes, comments, and shares.

The application processes aggregated video performance metrics only. It does not retrieve individual comments, commenter usernames, follower profiles, or private messages. Features for interacting with other users are not part of the current application.

4. How Data Is Used

Data is used to track video performance, maintain analytics records, and compare content performance over time. The application does not require access to private messages.

5. Third-Party Services

AEVION Social Analytics uses Google Apps Script to automate the retrieval and processing of authorized social media analytics data.

Notion is used as a private dashboard for storing historical video performance statistics and organizing analytics records.

Data processed through these services may be transferred to or processed in countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States.

The application also relies on the official APIs of connected social media platforms, subject to their respective authorization and privacy rules.

6. Data Retention and Security

Historical video performance statistics are stored in a private Notion dashboard for long-term analytics and performance tracking.

Disconnecting a social media account stops future data collection from that account. Previously collected analytics records remain stored in Notion until they are manually deleted.

Access credentials are kept separate from publicly accessible website content. Access to the analytics dashboard is restricted to the application operator.

Stored analytics records can be deleted by contacting the application operator at oliver.nestmann@proton.me. Personal data will be deleted when required by applicable law.

7. Your Rights

Individuals may object to processing based on legitimate interests and may withdraw consent at any time where consent is the legal basis for processing.

Individuals also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

Privacy inquiries and deletion requests can be sent to oliver.nestmann@proton.me.
